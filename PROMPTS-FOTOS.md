## Estilo base (pégalo al inicio de CADA prompt)

> Photorealistic architectural photography, contemporary Yucatán hacienda-style house in Mérida, Mexico. Pale cream limestone walls, dark slatted wood, rough stone wall, travertine floors, lush tropical plants (palms, elephant ears), warm golden-hour light with soft dappled shadows, high-end editorial look, natural colors. No people, no text, no logos, no watermark, no signs.

Para mantener coherencia, en ChatGPT sube `assets/adcom-hero.png` como referencia y agrega: "mismo estilo, misma casa y materiales que la imagen de referencia".

## Prompts (después del estilo base)

1. **Llegada** — Point of view from inside a car driving slowly along a limestone-paved private driveway toward the house, seen through the windshield; the dark edge of the hood barely visible at the bottom of the frame. A tall wooden entrance gate stands open, the low contemporary house with a large stone-framed doorway is ahead, tropical trees line the drive, soft late-afternoon light. Wide 16:9.
2. **Fachada** — Front facade of the house at dusk, blue-hour sky, warm interior lights glowing through a large slatted wood panel and a deep entrance portal, rough stone garden wall, reflection on a thin water feature in the foreground. Wide 16:9.
3. **Interior** — Open-plan living room with double-height ceiling, wooden beams, linen sofa, travertine floor, floor-to-ceiling openings to a tropical courtyard with a pool, morning light. Wide 16:9.
4. **Detalle** — Close-up architectural detail of rough stone wall meeting dark vertical wood slats and a plaster edge, strong raking sunlight, deep shadows, a leaf shadow on the stone. Wide 16:9.
5. **Cocina** — Contemporary kitchen and dining area with a long stone island, handmade ceramic pieces, wooden dining table, pendant woven lamp, open to a garden, soft daylight. 4:3.
6. **Recámara** — Serene bedroom with plaster walls, low platform bed with natural linen, wooden slat headboard wall, a tall window onto palm leaves, warm morning light. Vertical 4:5.
7. **Terraza de noche** — Outdoor terrace and narrow pool at night, warm uplighting on the stone wall and palms, wooden lounge furniture, glowing reflections on the water, deep blue sky. Wide 16:9.
8. **Aérea** — Aerial drone view of the contemporary house with an internal courtyard, long pool, flat roofs and a surrounding tropical garden in Yucatán, golden light. Wide 16:9.
9. **Obra** — A residential house under construction in Mérida: exposed concrete structure, stacked limestone blocks, rebar, wooden formwork and scaffolding, clean organized site, afternoon sun, no workers. 4:3.
10. **Maqueta** — Architect's desk with a white architectural scale model of a courtyard house, rolled blueprints, a pencil and scale ruler, a material sample board with limestone, wood and plaster, soft natural light from a window. No readable text. 4:3.
11. **Mantenimiento** — A well-kept house exterior: freshly painted cream plaster wall, trimmed garden, clean stone path, a watering hose neatly coiled, healthy palms, crisp morning light, a sense of care and upkeep. No people. 4:3.
12. **Arcos** — A long corridor of cream plaster arches opening onto a tropical garden, clay-tile floor, light and shadow patterns, a single wooden door at the end. Vertical 4:5.

## Notas
- Pide 1 imagen por prompt y, si no te gusta el resultado, repite con "misma escena, otra variación".
- Evita que salgan personas, letreros o marcas: son imágenes conceptuales, no obras reales de ADCOM.
- Conserva la leyenda "imagen conceptual" en el sitio mientras no sean obras autorizadas.
